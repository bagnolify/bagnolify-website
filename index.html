The full Bagnolify treatment, for a Vietnamese bakery-restaurant group in San Francisco and Berkeley. We built the website and menu, then put that same menu live across six ordering platforms. We ran the marketing — Meta campaigns with local creators on a $50-review program, plus printed posters we graded against camera footage: who stopped, who walked in.

Behind the counter, we automated the books. Two pieces of software — Xero Stage 0 and Stage 1 — scan every physical receipt, extract the invoice data, and post each expense to the P&L. A third, Platform Profits, pulls each platform’s monthly sales, nets out commissions and tax, and files those too. We installed sixteen security cameras across locations, wired to the office TVs and the owner’s phone. And when the owner began selling his perfected business model to new operators, we built the franchise line: bread and meat equipment, recipes, training, and onboarding — all purchasable from the site.
